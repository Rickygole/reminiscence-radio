# Requirements

1. The home page shows a large walnut tube radio with grille, brass trim, glowing dial, five glass tubes, a VU meter and a knob.
2. Turning or pressing the knob warms the tubes, sweeps the dial to Rose's station and plays the show.
3. The show plays host lines, a family voice message with a photo, songs, a story and reminders in the order given by show.json.
4. A segment that fails to play is skipped and the show continues.
5. Turning the knob back to off stops everything from any state.
6. The Control Room replays the crew run from events.json and never plays sound.
7. The Caregiver page lists today's segments, the safety report, an editor for reminders and the simple mode and captions settings.
8. Reminder edits are stored on the device, trimmed, stripped of control characters and capped at 200 characters.
9. The site works with no credentials and no network beyond its own files.
