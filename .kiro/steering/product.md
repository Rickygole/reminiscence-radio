# Product steering

Reminiscence Radio is a website shaped like a tabletop tube radio for Rose, a fictional listener born in 1946. One big dial plays a daily show: a host voice, recorded family messages with a photo, gentle songs from her teenage years, an on this day story and caregiver reminders word for word.

- Every page works with zero credentials. The bundled demo show is always available.
- The radio shows only what the crew published. The crew lives in a separate repository and shares two JSON schemas with this one.
- Placeholders are labelled as placeholders. Nothing pretends to be a real family recording.
