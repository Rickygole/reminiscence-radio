# Calm design steering

- Everything moves slowly and softly. Nothing flashes more than three times per second; test/meter.test.js enforces this.
- Simple mode and prefers-reduced-motion turn off tilt, static, dial sweep, moving photo and view transitions. Tubes then glow steadily. The meter needle still moves.
- Captions are on by default, in a large serif inside the dial window, mirrored to an aria-live region.
- Body text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast. Focus rings are 3 px. Every control works from the keyboard. The power knob has a hit target of at least 160 px.
- Palette: cream #F3E9D2, mustard #D9A441, teal #3E7C7B, walnut #5A3A22.
