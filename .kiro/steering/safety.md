# Safety steering

- Rose is fictional. No real patient data is used.
- Not medical advice. Reminders are the caregiver's words, played exactly as written.
- No voice is ever cloned. Family messages are recordings. Only the host, stories and reminders use computer speech.
- Songs are made by code in src/radio/song-synth.js and are CC0.
- The show loader is the trust boundary: unknown versions, unknown segment kinds and unusable audio never reach the player.
