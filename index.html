About this radio

  • Rose is a fictional listener. No real patient data is used.
  • The host is a computer voice. Family messages are recordings made by the people who sent them. Right now the voice and photo are placeholders.
  • The songs are made by code written for this project and are free to use (CC0).
  • This is not medical advice. Caregiver reminders play exactly as the caregiver wrote them.
  • No voice is ever cloned.
